❌ Warning Signs You Missed

  • 🚩
    Suspicious Domain: The URL was not from adobe.com. Always verify the actual domain, not just the display name or logo.
  • 🚩
    Urgency Tactics: "Expires in 24 hours" creates artificial pressure to act quickly without thinking.
  • 🚩
    Credential Request: Legitimate services don't ask for email verification to view documents you were already sent.
  • 🚩
    Unexpected Request: Did you actually expect an RFP or bid invitation? Attackers rely on "maybe it's real" thinking.
  • 🚩
    Professional Appearance: Modern phishing emails look legitimate. Design quality is no longer a reliable indicator.

✅ How to Protect Yourself

  • ✓
    Verify the sender through official channels before clicking links or downloading attachments
  • ✓
    Hover over links (without clicking) to see the actual destination URL
  • ✓
    Check the domain carefully - Look for typos like adobee.com or ad0be.com
  • ✓
    Be skeptical of urgency - Legitimate businesses don't use high-pressure tactics
  • ✓
    Never enter credentials on sites you arrived at via email links
  • ✓
    Report suspicious emails to your IT security team immediately
  • ✓
    When in doubt, verify - Contact the sender through official contact information
